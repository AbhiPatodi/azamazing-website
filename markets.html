Dual Entity Structure

Why Two Entities?

Operating separate legal entities in the UK and US gives us the ability to trade correctly within each jurisdiction's regulatory and tax framework.

Regulatory Compliance

Each entity complies with the tax and business laws of its respective country — HMRC in the UK, IRS and state authorities in the US — keeping all operations fully above board.

Credible Distributor Accounts

When approaching a UK distributor, we present our UK Ltd credentials. For US distributors, we use our LLC. This builds immediate trust and opens legitimate wholesale accounts.

Independent Market Operations

The UK business sells on Amazon.co.uk; the US business sells on Amazon.com. Each operates as a standalone retailer with its own supply chain and marketplace account.

Full Legal Standing

We can provide certificates of incorporation, company numbers, tax IDs, and registered addresses to any distributor or supplier requiring verification.

Contact us directly — we are ready to provide all required business documentation.
